Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitchen Unity ("we," "us," or "our"), operator of the Kitchen Unity platform (the "Service"), collects, uses, and protects information. Kitchen Unity is a multi-tenant CRM used by cabinet retailers to manage sales leads, measurements, and related business operations.

1. Who this policy covers

Kitchen Unity serves two groups of people, and the way we handle data differs for each:

  • Our customers (store operators & staff) — the businesses and users who log in to operate their workspace.
  • Leads (end consumers) — individuals who submit their contact details to one of our customers, for example through a Facebook Lead Ad. For this data, our customer is the "data controller" and we act as a "data processor" on their behalf.

2. Information we collect

CategoryExamplesSource
Account information Name, email address, password (hashed), role, store affiliation Provided by our customers at sign-up
Lead information Name, email address, phone number, lead source and status Submitted by consumers via Facebook Lead Ads or entered by staff
Business records Measurements, quotes, orders, and related notes Created by our customers within the Service
Technical data Log data, session identifiers, and basic usage information Collected automatically to operate and secure the Service

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the Service.
  • To authenticate users and manage workspace access.
  • To receive and de-duplicate sales leads from connected sources such as Facebook Lead Ads.
  • To provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Facebook Lead Ads integration

When one of our customers connects a Facebook Page, Kitchen Unity receives lead form submissions from Meta through the Facebook Lead Ads API. We retrieve only the fields the consumer submitted through the ad form (such as name, email, and phone number) and store them against the customer's workspace so the customer can follow up. We use this data solely to deliver leads to the customer who owns the corresponding Facebook Page, and for no other purpose. Use of information received from Meta follows Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

5. Browser extension

We offer an optional Chrome extension that assists authorized staff with their lead workflow. It processes only the data needed for that workflow and transmits it to the Kitchen Unity platform over a secure connection. It does not sell data, does not use it for advertising, and does not collect browsing activity unrelated to its stated purpose.

6. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Service providers — infrastructure and hosting partners (including our database and authentication provider) that process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Our customers — lead and business data is made available to the customer workspace it belongs to.
  • Legal requirements — where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

7. Data storage and security

Data is stored with our hosting provider in the United States. We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit, access controls, and per-tenant data isolation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as an account is active or as needed to provide the Service, and thereafter as required to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements (up to 24 months after account closure). Customers may request deletion of lead data at any time.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we process lead data on behalf of our customers, requests from consumers are generally directed to the relevant customer; we will assist our customers in responding. To exercise a right or ask a question, contact us using the details below.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by additional notice.
